# 10-minute test session kit: SIP Back on Track (P3–P5)

**Link to share:** https://mvp-sip.vercel.app (use only this link)
**Who:** someone who started a SIP in the last 12–18 months. Ideally they've missed or had a failed instalment.
**Before you start:** note the **time** (to find their records in the log later).

---

## 1. Read out (30 s)
"This is a prototype help tool for a Groww-style app. The SIP on screen is made up. I'm testing the tool, not you. Please type in **your own words**, not the example buttons, and think out loud."

## 2. Three tasks (about 2 minutes each)
Read the situation, then let them work. Don't help.

**Task 1: failed instalment**
> "It's the 9th. Your bank texted that your SIP debit failed because your balance was low. Your salary comes on the 10th. Find out what to do."
*(If they want, they can start by tapping the notification at the top.)*

**Task 2: a change to the SIP**
> "Your SIP date is the 5th, but your salary comes on the 10th. Or, if they prefer: you can't pay next month because of a trip. Find your options."

**Task 3: fund advice**
> "Ask it for a fund to invest in, the way you'd ask a friend."

After each task, ask: **"What would you do now?"**
- Says the right step without looking → **Y**
- Vague, or looks back at the screen → **Partial**
- Wrong or gives up → **N**

## 3. Five questions at the end (about 2 minutes)
1. What would you do if this happened to your real SIP tomorrow?
2. What made you trust or not trust the answers? *(Score 1–5)*
3. Was anything confusing or missing?
4. How did you feel when it wouldn't recommend a fund?
5. How likely are you to use this if Groww had it? *(1–5)*

---

